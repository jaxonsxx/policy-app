# ASA Policy App

## Overview

The Augustana Students' Association (ASA) has numerous operating policies in various states of revision and drafting. The various Google Drives have many copies of the same policies, with no clear indication which is the most current and up to date version.

## Issues

It can be difficult:

- For ASA members and constituents to find current policies
- To know when policies were last updated
- To track annual reviews of policies
- To understand current policy

## Project Description

The project is to design a software system that helps the ASA effectively broadcast, explain, and manage its policies and bylaws. The system will provide a centralized, searchable repository of official policy documents alongside clearly labeled plain-language summaries, visual explanations, and contextual FAQs that improve student understanding without altering the authoritative text.

It will support policy lifecycle tracking (e.g., draft, approved, amended), targeted announcements when policies change.

## Users

The app will have 2 intended levels of users:

### 1. Public View Access

- Able to see all current policies and some revision details (time, revised by)

### 2. Edit Access

- Members of the ASA Council, Policy Working Group
- Subject to change every year
- Add, delete, change, and approve policies
- See previous versions of policy for reference
- View comments of previous private access users

## App Features (Required)

The following features for the app are needed:

- **Policy Repository**
  - Policy Number
  - Policy Purpose (overview of policy intention)
  - Policy (Text)
  - Tagging/Categories of Policy (eg. Student Clubs, Budget, etc.)
  - Policy Timeline (Last updated, created, etc.)
  - Policy statuses (eg. draft, approved, archived, under review)
- **Search function for Repository**

## Additional App Features (Dream Scenario)

- **Development of an FAQ** that showcases the most common questions in plain English and directly references the applicable policy. These FAQs would be added over time by Edit Access Users
- **Feedback module** for Public View Access
